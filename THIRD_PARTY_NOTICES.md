## Ethernet: lwIP (default) or CycloneTCP (GPL)

**By default, Ethernet support uses lwIP** (BSD-3-Clause, see
[thirdparty/ethernet/lwip/README.md](thirdparty/ethernet/lwip/README.md)): it can be used in closed-source and
commercial firmware. Its one condition: firmware distributed in binary form must reproduce lwIP's copyright notice,
conditions and disclaimer ([COPYING](thirdparty/ethernet/lwip/COPYING)) in its documentation or other materials.

**CycloneTCP Open (Oryx Embedded SARL, GNU General Public License version 2 or later)** is still included and can be
selected with `MIKROSDK_NET_STACK=CYCLONETCP` until 31 December 2026. Firmware that uses it is subject to the GPL,
whichever mikroSDK licence you use; if you cannot meet the GPL's terms, for example for closed-source firmware, you need
a commercial CycloneTCP licence from Oryx Embedded (<https://www.oryx-embedded.com>). mikroSDK does not accept the GPL
on your behalf: a project that uses CycloneTCP stops with a compile error until it defines `GPL_LICENSE_TERMS_ACCEPTED`
itself. How to do that, and the changes MIKROE made to CycloneTCP, are in
[thirdparty/ethernet/cyclonetcp/README.md](thirdparty/ethernet/cyclonetcp/README.md).

## Software bill of materials

Every release has a CycloneDX SBOM, `sbom.cdx.json`, in the package and as a release asset. It lists mikroSDK and
the components below with their versions, licences, sources and file hashes; it is generated by `scripts/sbom.py` from
[thirdparty/components.json](thirdparty/components.json).

## Changes made by MIKROE

- `scripts/import_lwip.py` imports lwIP and renames the identifier `code` to `_code` in it (`code` is a reserved word
  in the mikroC compilers); the changed files carry a notice and are listed in
  [thirdparty/ethernet/lwip/UPSTREAM.json](thirdparty/ethernet/lwip/UPSTREAM.json). `thirdparty/ethernet/lwip/port` is
  MIKROE's configuration.
- At release time, `scripts/keyword_replace.py` renames the identifier `code` to `_code` in the CycloneTCP and LVGL
  sources (`code` is a reserved word in the mikroC compilers) and adds a dated notice to every file it changes.
- CycloneTCP files changed in this repository carry a "Modified by MikroElektronika" notice in their header; the list
  is in [thirdparty/ethernet/cyclonetcp/README.md](thirdparty/ethernet/cyclonetcp/README.md).
- `thirdparty/lvgl/lv_conf` and the TinyUSB `callbacks`, `config` and `isr` folders are MIKROE configuration and glue.
