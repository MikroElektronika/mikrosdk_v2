### <font color=red>RENESAS</font>

**USB support added!**

USB support through the third-party [tinyUSB](https://github.com/hathach/tinyusb) driver is now available for Renesas RA MCUs with USBFS module.

+ RA2 MCUs
  + RA2A1, RA2L2
+ RA4 MCUs
  + RA4E1, RA4E2, RA4L1, RA4M1, RA4M2, RA4M3, RA4W1
+ RA6 MCUs
  + RA6E1, RA6E2, RA6M1, RA6M2, RA6M3, RA6M4, RA6M5, RA6T3
+ RA8 MCUs
  + RA8D1, RA8E1, RA8E2, RA8M1, RA8T1
  + RA8D2, RA8M2, RA8P1, RA8T2 (single core only)
